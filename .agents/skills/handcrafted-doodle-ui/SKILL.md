# Handcrafted & Doodle-Inspired UI Design System

This skill guides the creation of warm, human, handcrafted, and doodle-infused interfaces.
It deliberately departs from cold, techy, dark-mode, or generic AI-generated SaaS aesthetics in favor of a personal, tactile, and mindful notebook experience.

---

## 1. Core Visual Principles (Anti-Tech / Anti-AI-Slop)

1. **Strictly Light / Warm Theme Only:**
   - Base backgrounds must feel like tactile paper, cream, linen, or parchment.
   - Primary: `#FAF7EE` (Rice paper), `#FBF9F5` (Warm Cream), `#F5F2EB` (Alabaster Linen).
   - Absolutely NO dark pitch blacks (`#000000`), NO cold blue/zinc dark modes, NO shiny purple/cyan neon glows.
2. **Ink & Graphite Contrast:**
   - Ink text: `#2C2A26` (Fountain pen charcoal) or `#1E1D1B` (Soft black ink), never `#000000`.
   - Secondary notes/annotations: `#6B665E` (Pencil graphite).
   - Accents: Sage green (`#4F6D58`) for income, Terracotta clay (`#C85A48`) for expenses, Mustard/Highlighter yellow (`#F9E976`) for highlights.
3. **Organic Hand-Drawn Borders (The Wobbly Effect):**
   - Use irregular border-radius for cards: `border-radius: 255px 15px 225px 15px/15px 225px 15px 255px;`
   - Use dashed/sketched or SVG pencil lines for divider rules.
   - Avoid razor-sharp 1px sterile gray borders.
4. **Handwriting & Hand-Lettering Typography:**
   - Display & Accents: Google Fonts `Caveat`, `Patrick Hand`, `Kalam`, `Indie Flower`, or `Architects Daughter`.
   - Tabular Numbers & Financial Data: Crisp readable font with `tabular-nums` (e.g. `Plus Jakarta Sans`, `Inter`, or `Space Mono`) to ensure IDR calculations remain 100% legible.
5. **Doodle Elements & Tactile Details:**
   - Hand-sketched SVG icons (doodle wallet, doodle coins, sketchy coffee cup, arrow scribbles).
   - Washi-tape card headers (pastel rotated banners).
   - Highlighter stroke backgrounds (`rough-notation` style).
   - Hand-stamped badges (e.g., circled "PAID" or "SAVED" stamps).
